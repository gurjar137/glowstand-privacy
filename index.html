Glowstand – Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026

Summary

Glowstand turns your phone into a bedside or desk display while it charges. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you set up stays on your device.

Data stored on your device

Your settings, to-do list, counters and the list of photos you picked are saved only in the app's private storage on your phone. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Location (optional)

Only when you tap "Use my location", the app reads your approximate location. The coordinates are sent over an encrypted connection to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) to get the weather forecast, and your phone's built-in geocoder may be used to show the city name. City names you type in the weather search are sent to Open-Meteo's geocoding service. We do not store or share your location anywhere else. You can use weather without location permission by searching for a city.

Photos (optional)

Photos are chosen with Android's system photo picker, so the app can only see the photos you select. They are displayed on your screen and never uploaded or shared.

Calendar (optional)

If you allow calendar access, upcoming events are read on your device to show the Agenda widget. Calendar data is never changed, stored elsewhere or sent off your device.

Notification access (optional)

Android requires notification access before an app can show and control music from other apps (e.g. Spotify, YouTube Music). Glowstand only uses it to read the song title, artist, artwork and playback state and to send play / pause / skip commands. The content of your notifications is never read, stored or shared.

Third parties

Weather data is provided by Open-Meteo. Their terms and privacy information: open-meteo.com/en/terms. No other third-party services receive data from this app.

Children

Glowstand is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in the app and on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to the developer email shown on Glowstand's Google Play listing.
